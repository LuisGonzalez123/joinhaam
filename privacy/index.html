Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026

This policy describes how HAAM (“we”, “us”) handles information when you use the HAAM mobile app and this website.

Information we collect

  • Account. Email address and password when you create an account, plus the one-time code we send to verify that email.
  • Activity in the app. Votes and other choices you make on bills and related content, and profile details you choose to add.
  • Feedback. A rating, the message you write, and a contact email if you include one.
  • Technical data. Basic device and app information needed to run the service, keep you signed in, and fix errors.

We do not ask you to sell personal information, and we do not use your data to sell ads.

How we use it

  • Create and secure your account.
  • Show your votes and the rest of the app.
  • Read and reply to feedback.
  • Keep the service reliable and protect it from abuse.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers that host the app, send verification email, or help us operate HAAM, and when the law requires it. We do not sell your personal information.

Retention

We keep account and activity data for as long as your account is open, and for a limited time afterward if we need it for security, disputes, or legal duties. Feedback is kept long enough to review and act on it.

Your choices

You can update account details in the app and you can ask us to delete your account. Email the address below and we will process that request.

Children

HAAM is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date.
